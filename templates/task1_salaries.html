Key Statistical Findings (Task 1 Report)

  • Cloud, Distributed Systems & AI Lead Compensation: Keywords such as Kubernetes, AWS, Machine Learning, LLM, and Rust command average hourly rates between $45 and $65/hr—an premium of over +50% to +100% compared to the market baseline ($29.62/hr).
  • Statistical Significance (ANOVA): The ANOVA $F$-statistic tests for high-premium keywords show $p < 0.001$, decisively rejecting the null hypothesis that salary differences are due to random noise.
  • Seniority Multipliers: Executive & Architect roles (Lead, Principal, Architect) average $49.80/hr, offering a +75% premium over Junior/Entry-level roles ($28.40/hr).
