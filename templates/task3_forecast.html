Predictive Modeling Architecture & Verification

1. Feature Engineering:

Autoregressive lag windows ($t-1$ to $t-7$), 3-day and 7-day rolling moving averages and standard deviations, day-of-week one-hot encoding, and weekend seasonality flags.

2. Out-of-Sample Holdout:

Strict chronological split ensuring zero lookahead bias. Evaluated against unseen future days using MAE, RMSE, MAPE, and $R^2$.

3. Prediction Intervals:

95% uncertainty bounds estimated via model residual standard errors scaled by multi-step error propagation factor $\sqrt{1 + 0.05 \cdot h}$.
